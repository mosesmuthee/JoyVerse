Dearest Joy,

Six months. When I think about it, it doesn't seem like a long time, yet somehow it feels like we've packed years' worth of conversations, lessons, laughter, prayers, and memories into those months. Looking back, I never could have imagined how much you would come to mean to me. You've challenged me to think differently, encouraged me when I needed it most, corrected me when I was wrong, and helped me see life from perspectives I would have otherwise missed. You have been a friend, a listener, a teacher, a source of wisdom, and one of the greatest blessings God has placed in my life this year. One thing that has always amazed me is how openly and genuinely you care. You've never been afraid to show your love and appreciation for me, whether in private conversations or even in public. Every time you call me "my love" so casually and naturally, every time you call me "munch," every time you check in on me, encourage me, pray for me, or simply remind me that I matter, it leaves a mark on my heart. Those may seem like small things, but to me, they carry so much meaning because they reflect the kind of person you are—someone who loves deeply, cares genuinely, and gives so much of herself to the people around her

One of my favorite things about these past six months is all the moments we've shared together. The random conversations that somehow become life lessons, the laughs that leave us smiling long after the conversation ends, the debates, the jokes, the encouragement, and the advice. But beyond the happy moments, I'm also grateful for the times that weren't easy. We've had moments where things weren't at their best, moments of misunderstanding, difficult conversations, frustrations, or seasons where things felt a little off. Yet somehow, every single time, we've come through stronger. Instead of letting those moments break what we had, we've allowed them to teach us, strengthen us, and bring us closer. That's something I deeply appreciate because it reminds me that what we have isn't built only on good days but also on the willingness to work through the difficult ones. You've created memories that I genuinely treasure, and you've become someone I value far more than you probably realize. Your kindness, your heart for God, your wisdom, your honesty, your humor, your strength, and even your unmatched fashion sense have all left a mark on me. I never want to take your presence, your friendship, or the time you've invested in me for granted because people like you are rare, and I'm thankful every day that our paths crossed.

As we celebrate six months of knowing each other, I find myself excited not only for what we've shared but for everything that still lies ahead. There are more conversations to have, more lessons to learn, more laughs to share, more memories to create, and more milestones to celebrate. No one knows exactly what the future holds, but I do know this: meeting you has been one of the greatest blessings of my year, and I thank God for every moment of this friendship. I love you, Joy—not simply because of what you've done for me, but because of who you are. I love your heart, your passion for God, the way you care for people, the wisdom you carry, the joy you bring into rooms, and the impact you've had on my life. You are incredibly precious to me, appreciated more than you know, and valued more than I probably say. Thank you for every memory, every prayer, every lesson, every correction, every laugh, every moment of support, and every moment you've chosen to walk alongside me during these past six months.

Happy Six Months, My Love. Here's to every memory behind us, every challenge we've overcome, every lesson we've learned, and the countless memories that still wait ahead of us.

With all my love,
